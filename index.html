03 / LIFECYCLE

  1. 01 · Define

    Make the question clear.

    State the outcomes, the deadline and the evidence that decides the result.

  2. 02 · Trade

    Put a view into the market.

    Trades move prices. A price is a signal, not a promise.

  3. 03 · Read

    Watch the signal emerge.

    The market price is the crowd's live estimate — readable at a glance, inspectable in depth.

  4. 04 · Resolve

    Return to the evidence.

    Reality.eth settles the answer against the question's public criteria.

  5. 05 · Settle

    Backstop and payout.

    Kleros arbitrates disputes; winners redeem. Decisions stay with the people responsible.
